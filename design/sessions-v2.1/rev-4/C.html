Keyboard: step rows are buttons

Focus ring on step 4 (shown above)
  • Tab moves through the step rows in order, then the composer.
  • Enter or Space selects the step; the inspector updates and announces the step title.
  • Phone width: Enter opens the bottom sheet and moves focus to its title; Esc closes it and returns focus to the row.
  • The running step (8) is not a button until its evidence exists.

Action annotations on the Before screenshot

The marker is drawn from the step's action (tap point, typed field, swipe path or hardware key) on the screenshot taken before the action. The line under the image names the action; the image label says it for screen readers.
